Guidance on Caesar Shift Ciphers:
A shift cipher replaces each letter with the letter found k positions ahead in the alphabet, wrapping around from Z back to A:
- Let A=0, B=1, ..., Z=25.
- Encryption with shift k: C = (P + k) mod 26.
- Decryption with shift k: P = (C - k) mod 26 = (C + (26 - k)) mod 26.
For ROT-13, k = 13 (so encryption and decryption are symmetric because 26 - 13 = 13).
For ROT-12, k = 12. Encryption adds 12 (mod 26); Decryption subtracts 12, which is equivalent to adding 14 (mod 26).
Ensure spaces, punctuation, and letter case remain unchanged.
